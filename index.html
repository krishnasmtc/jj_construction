WHEN WAS AJ CONSTRUCTION FOUNDED?

Established in 2004, AJ Construction has expanded from a specialized civil engineering contractor into a comprehensive EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) leader delivering prominent commercial complexes and national infrastructure projects nationwide.

WHAT ACCREDITATIONS & PERMITS DO YOU MAINTAIN?

We hold full state contractor licensing, active OSHA safety certifications, and current building permits across every municipality we operate in. Our senior engineers carry LEED Accredited Professional and PE credentials, and every site undergoes third-party inspection at each major project milestone.

DO YOU OFFER FIXED-COST GUARANTEES?

Yes. Once scope and drawings are finalized, we issue a fixed-price contract with no hidden change orders. Our estimators build in contingency at the bidding stage, so the price you sign is the price you pay barring client-requested scope changes.
